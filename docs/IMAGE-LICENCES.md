## Representation Rules

* No stock subject is presented as a cancer patient, survivor, client or testimonial source.
* The distant walker is a general image of movement and reflection only and must not be represented as a cancer patient or survivor.
* Food imagery represents everyday nourishment, not a treatment, prevention or cure claim.
* The book and tea image represents reflection, not medical advice.
* Do not introduce staged illness, hospital distress, pink ribbons, fake survivor imagery, stock doctors or literal healing crystals.
