# Thrive Through Cancer Website Design

Date: 15/07/2026

## Objective

Create a calm, credible and conversion focused website for Thrive Through Cancer, a division of Inheritance Academy. The site must support people affected by cancer without resembling a hospital, a generic wellness brand, or a template driven coaching website.

## Approved Direction

The approved direction is **Quiet Vitality**. It combines editorial typography, generous spacing, warm portraiture and a restrained teal palette. The emotional tone is reassuring and capable. Hope is expressed through clarity and partnership, not exaggerated claims.

## Audience

The primary audiences are:

1. People who have recently received a cancer diagnosis.
2. People undergoing treatment or entering survivorship.
3. Family members and caregivers seeking informed support.
4. People interested in cancer prevention and sustainable lifestyle change.

## Information Architecture

The first release is a focused single page experience:

1. Hero and immediate booking path.
2. Audience recognition and emotional reassurance.
3. Renny's biography and qualifications.
4. Four core support services.
5. Radical Remission, Functional Medicine and PEMS methodology.
6. Coaching and counselling comparison.
7. Transparent pricing and financial accessibility.
8. Booking request and payment method selection.
9. Medical disclaimer, privacy links and organisation relationship.

Repeated booking prompts from the source copy are consolidated into a consistent call to action system.

## Visual Language

The palette uses deep teal, clear aqua, warm cream, mineral sand and charcoal. Editorial serif headings create warmth and distinction. A legible humanist sans serif supports longer reading. A custom gem motif references PEMS without inventing certification marks or clinical symbols.

Photography is intentionally personal. The seated portrait anchors the hero because it offers eye contact and warmth. The standing portrait supports the biography and credentials section.

## Interaction Design

Motion is limited to soft reveal sequences, small button movement and a restrained hero image drift. All motion respects reduced motion preferences. Navigation remains usable by keyboard, focus states remain visible and mobile navigation uses a labelled button.

## Booking And Payment

The interface supports Foundation Sessions, Follow Up Sessions and the twelve session package. It also presents PayFast, card and EFT payment preferences. Production submission and payment processing remain isolated integration points until the client supplies booking availability, contact details, PayFast credentials, banking instructions and legal wording.

## Content Safeguards

Coaching and counselling are positioned as complementary support. The site explicitly states that it does not replace medical diagnosis, treatment or emergency mental health care. Statistical and Radical Remission claims require final source verification before production publication.

## Quality Standard

The release must be responsive at desktop, tablet and mobile widths. It must pass checks for horizontal overflow, missing images, keyboard navigation, accessible form labels, visible focus, reduced motion, semantic headings and truthful booking states.
