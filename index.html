Meet Renny, your guide and partner in healing

A guide who sees the whole person

Clinical insight. Human warmth. A plan made with you.

I am Renny, a health coach and psycho-oncology counsellor dedicated to helping people navigate cancer with resilience and hope.

My approach brings together functional medicine principles, lifestyle change and psycho-oncology techniques. Whether you are newly diagnosed, in treatment or living with uncertainty, we work with the emotional and practical realities of your life, not a generic checklist.

I believe every person carries strength and resilience. Our work helps you access those resources, build self efficacy and take an active role in your wellbeing.

The PEMS Conditioning Gems

A personalised view of what supports your healing

Our flagship PEMS tool looks across four connected dimensions of wellbeing: Physical, Emotional, Mental and Spiritual.

Twelve conditioning gems, informed by extensive cancer research, help identify where support may have the greatest value. Your assessment becomes a practical, individual guide for coaching, not a score or diagnosis.

Radical Remission

Research informed factors that complement care

Dr Kelly Turner's research explored people who experienced healing beyond medical projections and identified ten recurring factors. We use these insights alongside PEMS and Functional Medicine coaching to support quality of life and positive change.

These approaches complement conventional cancer care and do not replace it.

Functional Medicine

Where biology, behaviour and daily life meet

Our coaching draws on root cause thinking, positive psychology, behavioural change, neuroscience and mind body practices such as breathwork, visualisation, grounding and meditation.

The aim is sustainable agency, not a perfect routine.
